What this does and doesn't tell you

  • Download/upload: real throughput to a nearby Cloudflare edge node over HTTPS. Reflects the entire transmission path: Wi-Fi link, LAN switches, firewalls, and ISP uplink.
  • Idle latency/jitter: round-trip time and variance measured while the link is otherwise quiet. High jitter with good throughput indicates RF airtime contention or congested channels.
  • Loaded latency (bufferbloat): delta latency under active saturation. A significant jump indicates excessive buffering on routers/firewalls rather than prioritization via WMM/QoS.
  • Not measured from browser: RSSI, SNR, channel width, MCS rate, spatial streams, or retry percentage. Browsers have no OS driver permission for this layer. Use the CLI commands below to pull them directly.

Pulling your own connection details

Platform Cara Mendapatkan Detail Sinyal RF
macOS Option-click ikon Wi-Fi di menu bar — menampilkan RSSI, Noise, Tx rate, PHY mode, MCS index, dan Channel secara native.
Windows netsh wlan show interfaces di Terminal — menampilkan signal %, radio type, channel, dan Tx/Rx link speed.
ArubaOS 8 (Network Side) show ap association client-mac <mac> — menampilkan controller-side view: SNR, RSSI, Tx/Rx drop rate, and negotiated MCS.
Aruba Central AOS 10 Menu Manage > Clients > Wireless Client Detail — dashboard Live RF, band (2.4/5/6 GHz), dan health timeline.

Aruba Wireless Troubleshooting Toolkit

Platform-implementation reference for network engineers managing Aruba OS 8 & Aruba Central OS 10.

Aruba Instant (IAP)

Virtual Controller (VC) terdistribusi. Forwarding dilakukan secara lokal (bridge) tanpa dependensi controller terpusat.

ArubaOS 8 Campus

Mobility Conductor (MCR) & Controller (MC). Traffic klien di-tunneling lewat GRE ke firewall PEF terpusat.

Aruba Central (AOS 10)

Cloud-native control plane microservices. Eliminasi Conductor fisik dengan opsi Bridge Mode dan Gateway Microbranch.

AP Conversion Playbook (IAP • CAP • Central AOS 10)

        
Panduan praktis konversi mode operasional antar arsitektur.

      

      

        

          
1. IAP → Campus AP (AOS 8 MC)

          
Eksekusi via CLI Virtual Controller:

          
IAP# convert-aos-ap cap <IP-Controller>

          
AP akan me-reboot dan menarik firmware image dari Mobility Controller.

        

        

          
2. Campus AP → IAP Standalone

          
Eksekusi melalui console bootloader:

          

            apboot> purgeenv

            apboot> clear os

            apboot> saveenv

            apboot> reset
          

        

        

          
3. IAP / CAP → Aruba Central AOS 10

          
Assign Foundation License di GreenLake & masukkan ke Group AOS 10. AP akan menghubungi Activate dan auto-upgrade ke image AOS 10.

        

        

          
4. Central AOS 10 Rollback → AOS 8

          
Lepas lisensi di portal GreenLake, pindahkan AP keluar grup di Central, lalu downgrade image firmware via console.

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
Auth-tracebuf Live Decoding (802.1X & ClearPass)

        
Perintah tercepat mengisolasi titik putus handshake EAP/RADIUS di controller.

      

      

        (MC-01) # show auth-tracebuf mac 48:2a:e3:11:22:33
      

      

        

          
server-timeout

          
RADIUS Server mati, port UDP 1812 diblokir, atau Shared Secret mismatch.

        

        

          
eap-failure

          
Password salah, akun AD locked, atau policy service di ClearPass tidak match.

        

        

          
client-timeout

          
Supplicant client menolak trust sertifikat EAP ClearPass (isu CA certificate).
