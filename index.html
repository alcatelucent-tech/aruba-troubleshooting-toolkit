            <h4 class="text-sm font-semibold text-slate-200 flex items-center gap-2">

              <span class="text-aruba-orange">1.</span> Urutan Discovery Controller (Discovery Order Hierarchy)

            </h4>

            <p class="text-xs text-slate-300 leading-relaxed">

              Saat AP booting dan mendapatkan IP DHCP, AP mencari Mobility Conductor (MC) atau Local Controller dengan urutan berikut:

            </p>

            <ol class="list-decimal list-inside text-xs text-slate-400 space-y-2 pl-2">

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">Static Environment:</strong> Variabel NVRAM tersimpan di AP (`master=&lt;ip&gt;`).</li>

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">DHCP Option 43:</strong> Vendor specific string berisi IP Controller.</li>

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">ADP (Aruba Discovery Protocol):</strong>

                <ul class="list-disc list-inside pl-5 mt-1 text-slate-400">

                  <li>Multicast: Mengirim frame ke IP <code class="text-amber-400 bg-slate-950 px-1 py-0.5 rounded">239.0.82.11</code>.</li>

                  <li>Broadcast: Mengirim UDP packet ke port <code class="text-amber-400 bg-slate-950 px-1 py-0.5 rounded">UDP 8211</code>.</li>

                </ul>

              </li>

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">DNS Resolution:</strong> Resolve query default: <code class="text-cyan-400 bg-slate-950 px-1 py-0.5 rounded">aruba-master.&lt;domain-name-dari-dhcp&gt;</code>.</li>

            </ol>

          </div>


          <!-- CPsec & Tunneling Card -->

          <div class="bg-slate-900 border border-slate-800 rounded-xl p-5 space-y-4">

            <h4 class="text-sm font-semibold text-slate-200 flex items-center gap-2">

              <span class="text-aruba-orange">2.</span> Control Plane Security (CPsec) & Port Firewall

            </h4>

            <p class="text-xs text-slate-300 leading-relaxed">

              Setelah IP Controller ditemukan, komunikasi control-plane wajib melewati enkripsi IPsec (CPsec). Jika CPsec aktif dan sertifikat/MAC AP belum disetujui (Approved), AP akan masuk status <code class="text-rose-400 font-mono">I (Inactive)</code> atau <code class="text-amber-400 font-mono">U (Unprovisioned)</code>.

            </p>

            <div class="bg-slate-950 p-3 rounded-lg border border-slate-800 font-mono text-xs text-slate-300 space-y-1 overflow-x-auto">

              <div class="text-slate-500"># Verifikasi IPsec SA dan Status CPsec di Mobility Conductor / Controller:</div>

              <div class="text-emerald-400">(MC-01) # show crypto ipsec sa</div>

              <div class="text-emerald-400">(MC-01) # show whitelist-db cpsec</div>

              <div class="text-emerald-400">(MC-01) # show ap database-summary</div>

            </div>

            <div class="text-xs text-slate-400">

              <strong class="text-slate-300">Port Firewall yang Wajib Terbuka antara AP dan Controller:</strong>

              <div class="grid grid-cols-2 sm:grid-cols-4 gap-2 mt-2 font-mono text-[11px]">

                <div class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-800 p-2 rounded text-center"><span class="text-slate-400">UDP 4500</span><br><span class="text-orange-400">IPsec NAT-T</span></div>

                <div class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-800 p-2 rounded text-center"><span class="text-slate-400">UDP 8211</span><br><span class="text-orange-400">PAPI Control</span></div>

                <div class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-800 p-2 rounded text-center"><span class="text-slate-400">GRE (IP 47)</span><br><span class="text-orange-400">User Datapath</span></div>

                <div class="bg-slate-950 border border-slate-800 p-2 rounded text-center"><span class="text-slate-400">TFTP/FTP</span><br><span class="text-orange-400">Image Sync</span></div>

              </div>

            <h4 class="text-sm font-semibold text-slate-200 flex items-center gap-2">

              <span class="text-cyan-400">1.</span> Alur Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP) Cloud

            </h4>

            <p class="text-xs text-slate-300 leading-relaxed">

              Pada AOS 10, tidak ada Mobility Master lokal. Seluruh AP bertindak sebagai cloud-managed device dan berkomunikasi langsung ke Aruba Central menggunakan enkripsi WebSocket/TLS.

            </p>

            <ol class="list-decimal list-inside text-xs text-slate-400 space-y-2 pl-2">

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">DHCP Initialization:</strong> AP wajib menerima IP, Default Gateway, DNS Server, dan resolusi NTP time yang valid.</li>

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">Koneksi ke Aruba Activate:</strong> AP melakukan HTTPS GET request ke <code class="text-cyan-400 bg-slate-950 px-1 py-0.5 rounded">activate.arubanetworks.com</code> (Port 443).</li>

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">Mutual TLS via TPM Chip:</strong> AP melakukan handshake menggunakan sertifikat hardware pabrik yang tersimpan di chip TPM.</li>

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">Redirect ke Central Cluster:</strong> Activate merespons dengan URL cluster Central tempat tenant berada (contoh: <code class="text-cyan-400 bg-slate-950 px-1 py-0.5 rounded">app-ap1.central.arubanetworks.com</code>).</li>

              <li><strong class="text-slate-200">Image Upgrade & Config Pull:</strong> Jika AP masih menjalankan AOS 8/Instant, Central memerintahkan upgrade ke image AOS 10 dan mengunduh konfigurasi Group.</li>

            </ol>

          </div>


          <div class="bg-slate-900 border border-slate-800 rounded-xl p-5 space-y-4">

            <h4 class="text-sm font-semibold text-slate-200 flex items-center gap-2">

              <span class="text-rose-400">2.</span> Titik Kegagalan Paling Sering (Common Failure Points AOS 10)

            </h4>

            <div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-3 text-xs">

              <div class="bg-slate-950 p-3 rounded-lg border border-slate-800">

                <div class="font-semibold text-rose-400 mb-1">NTP Desynchronization (Clock Skew)</div>

                <p class="text-slate-400">Jika jam AP selisih > 5 menit dari waktu UTC, handshake TLS ke Activate atau Central akan di-drop secara otomatis (Certificate Date Invalid).</p>

              </div>

              <div class="bg-slate-950 p-3 rounded-lg border border-slate-800">

                <div class="font-semibold text-rose-400 mb-1">Unassigned License / Group</div>

                <p class="text-slate-400">AP sudah muncul di *GreenLake Device Inventory*, tetapi belum diberi *Foundation/Advanced License* atau belum dimasukkan ke Group AOS 10.</p>

              </div>

              <div class="bg-slate-950 p-3 rounded-lg border border-slate-800">

                <div class="font-semibold text-rose-400 mb-1">Egress Firewall Blocking Outbound 443</div>

                <p class="text-slate-400">Firewall kantor/cabang memblokir HTTPS Inspection atau membatasi port outbound TCP 443 ke subnet cloud Aruba.</p>

              </div>

              <div class="bg-slate-950 p-3 rounded-lg border border-slate-800">

                <div class="font-semibold text-rose-400 mb-1">Stuck in Conversion Loop</div>

                <p class="text-slate-400">AP gagal download image AOS 10 karena MTU bottleneck atau drop saat transfer image dari Cloud Repository.</p>

              </div>

            </div>

            <div class="bg-slate-950 p-3 rounded-lg border border-slate-800 font-mono text-xs text-slate-300 space-y-1">

              <div class="text-slate-500"># Diagnostic Command melalui AP Console / Central Console:</div>

              <div class="text-cyan-400">AP# show central status</div>

              <div class="text-cyan-400">AP# show activate status</div>

              <div class="text-cyan-400">AP# show ap-env</div>

            </div>
